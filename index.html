Research

Researcher and lecturer working at the intersection of applied mathematics and artificial intelligence. My research spans Bayesian and causal reasoning, open-set and domain-generalizing deep learning, and physics-informed hybrid methods for decision support in complex industrial, energy and embedded systems.

Over 15 years across CNRS, Polytech, the University of Lille, Institut Mines-Télécom, Aix-Marseille University, the University of Oulu, the University of South Brittany and Halmstad University, I have led and contributed to competitive research grants as Principal Investigator or Co-Principal Investigator (see Projects for details), published 60+ peer-reviewed works across journals, conferences and technical reports, and taught and coordinated courses from first-cycle programming to postgraduate Bayesian statistics for machine learning (93% student evaluation). I hold a Ph.D. in Engineering Sciences (spécialité Productique) (LARIS, Polytech Angers, University of Angers, 2011–2015) and am qualified by the French National Scientific Committee for associate professor positions in applied mathematics, computer engineering, control and signal processing.

Fault detection and diagnosis is fundamentally a mechatronics problem: it depends on sensing, actuation and control, and a decision-support method is only useful if it can act through that layer. I am extending this hybrid, explainable-AI approach into multi-modal, federated and robotics/mechatronic settings working with real-time sensor and actuator data.

Green Bridge Island

2023–2024

Principal Investigator · Interreg Öresund-Kattegat-Skagerrak (ref. I20364015)

Connecting the electricity grids of two neighbouring countries requires close cooperation between Denmark and Sweden's energy companies and regulators; this pre-project with Øresundsbro Konsortiet identified the key stakeholders for that cooperation and demonstrated the benefits AI/ML can bring to critical energy challenges. Concretely: evaluating a 6.5 MW battery installation at the Öresund Bridge alongside its 2.5 GWh of annual solar production, and designing the AI approach for optimising when that storage is charged, discharged or held in reserve using predictive models and risk-aware cost-benefit analysis. As sole PI, I led the project from proposal to delivery. Total budget €100,000; accepted with a follow-on commissioned contract.
